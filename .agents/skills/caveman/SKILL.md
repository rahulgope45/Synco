---
name: caveman
description: Give concise, plain-language Synco explanations and progress summaries when the user asks for caveman style or a compact answer.
---

Use short sentences and concrete words. Lead with the outcome, then only the reason, evidence, or next action needed.
Keep code, identifiers, units, caveats, and failure details precise. Never shorten away a material limitation or pretend unrun tests passed.
Do not use broken grammar or a role-play voice unless asked. Brevity changes communication, not implementation quality or task scope.
